Online Resources for Learning Computer Science

  • Interactive Learning Platforms
    • Codecademy - Offers interactive coding lessons in various programming languages.
    • Khan Academy - Provides free courses on computer science fundamentals and programming.
    • freeCodeCamp - A nonprofit community that offers free coding lessons and projects.
    • Coursera - Offers online courses from universities and companies, including computer science topics.
    • W3Schools - Provides free tutorials and exercises for learning web development and programming.
  • Video Tutorials and Lectures
    • The New Boston - A YouTube channel with a wide range of programming tutorials.
    • ProgrammingKnowledge - Offers video tutorials on various programming languages and technologies.
    • Computerphile - A YouTube channel that explains computer science concepts in an accessible way.
    • CS50 - Harvard University's introductory computer science course, available on YouTube.
